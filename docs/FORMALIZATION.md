# Formalization scope

## Mathematical contract

`CubeSections.E n` is `EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n)`, and `Frame n k` is a real linear isometry from `E k` to `E n`. The cube has side length one. Section volume is the real value of Lebesgue measure of its preimage in `E k`.

`Semantics.lean` proves that this mass is finite and positive and agrees with normalized Euclidean Hausdorff measure on the section. All four target statements cover every `1 ≤ k ≤ n`. The upper bound and equality classification quantify over every such frame; attainment asserts the existence of an attaining balanced frame. No nonzero-row assumption is imposed on the public theorem.

The balanced-range condition uses a surjective assignment of ambient coordinates to blocks, an independent sign for each coordinate, and equality of the resulting coordinate-block plane with `Set.range U`. Block sizes are `n / k` or `n / k + 1`; their sum is `n`, which determines the number of large blocks. The condition is invariant under changing the domain's orthonormal basis.

## Proof organization

The geometric range uses strong induction on section dimension `k`, with actual fibers of dimension `k - 2`. A separate point-fiber argument handles `k = 2`; the public theorem is never invoked at dimension zero. The remaining classical codimension range is proved through the strict scalar sinc inequality, the complementary Fourier bound, and equality rigidity.

Intermediate reduction theorems retain their stated hypotheses. `Main.lean` supplies the required proved inputs and exports the unconditional statements. A proposition definition or an intermediate conditional theorem is not counted as an unconditional proof of that proposition.

The analytic transport theorem is proved for the regular probability laws actually generated by the frame construction. This suffices for the main theorem; a separate theorem for every arbitrary probability law is not asserted here. The functional Brascamp–Lieb input is proved for the class required by the sinc argument.

## Coverage

The formal claim of this repository is the main volume bound, existence of an extremizer, and full equality classification. The manuscript's additional stability and application sections are included as part of the mathematical reference, but this repository makes no blanket claim that every statement in those sections is formalized.

The general interval-coincidence statement for arbitrary translated slabs is likewise broader than the cube equality route used here. The cube classification obtains the required row-length rigidity through the proved row-variation and endpoint arguments.

## Trust boundary

The allowed foundational axioms are:

```lean
propext
Classical.choice
Quot.sound
```

The final targets have proof bodies and do not use admitted proofs, custom mathematical axioms, or `native_decide`. Python arithmetic checks support reproduction and data inspection; the formal certificate result additionally uses Lean soundness and coverage theorems and the proved link to the geometric inequality.

Recorded external checking, when present in `verification/`, refers to the actual exported types and proof bodies. A hash or an imported axiom summary alone is not an external type check. A successful external check does not by itself establish a compiler bootstrap or source/object correspondence for every cached dependency definition.
